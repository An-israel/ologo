# Privacy policy

This policy explains how [TO CONFIRM: legal business name] ("Ornis", "we") uses your personal data when you visit or buy from ornis.shop. We are the data controller. Contact: [TO CONFIRM: support email], [TO CONFIRM: business address].

## What we collect

- **Order details:** your name, delivery and billing address, email, phone number (optional), the items you buy and your order history.
- **Payment:** handled by Shopify Payments, PayPal and Klarna. We do not see or store your full card details.
- **Messages:** what you send us through the contact form, chat or email.
- **Marketing:** your email address, if you sign up to our emails.
- **Device and usage data:** collected through cookies, only with your consent for analytics and marketing (see our Cookie policy).

## Why we use it, and our lawful basis

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Processing and delivering your order, returns and refunds | Contract |
| Sending order and shipping emails | Contract |
| Answering your messages | Legitimate interests |
| Preventing fraud | Legitimate interests and legal obligation |
| Marketing emails | Consent (you can unsubscribe at any time) |
| Analytics and advertising cookies | Consent |
| Keeping accounting records | Legal obligation |

## Who we share it with

- **Shopify**, which hosts our shop and checkout.
- **Payment providers:** Shopify Payments, PayPal, Klarna.
- **Our suppliers and their couriers**, which receive your name, delivery address and phone number to send your order. Some suppliers are outside the UK. Where data leaves the UK we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.
- **Reviews:** Judge.me, which sends review requests after delivery.
- **Advertising and analytics:** Google, Meta and TikTok, only if you accept cookies.

We never sell your personal data.

## How long we keep it

Order records: 6 years for tax purposes. Marketing: until you unsubscribe. Messages: 2 years.

You have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email [TO CONFIRM: support email]. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
