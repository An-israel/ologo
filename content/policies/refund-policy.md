# Refund and returns policy

This policy applies to orders placed at ornis.shop by customers in the United Kingdom. It does not affect your legal rights.

## Changing your mind

You can cancel your order and return unworn, unwashed items with their labels attached within **14 days** of the day you receive them. You do not need to give a reason.

To start a return, contact us at [TO CONFIRM: support email] with your order number. We will reply with the return instructions and the return address.

You must send the items back within 14 days of telling us you want to cancel. [TO CONFIRM: who pays return postage, for example "You pay the cost of returning items unless they are faulty or wrong."] Please keep your proof of postage.

## Refunds

We refund you within 14 days of receiving the items back (or of you showing proof that you sent them, if that is earlier). We refund the price you paid and, if you return the whole order, the standard delivery charge. If you chose express delivery, we refund the cost of standard delivery only. Refunds go to your original payment method, including Klarna and PayPal.

We may reduce the refund if an item has been handled more than you would in a shop and lost value as a result.

## Items we cannot accept back

For hygiene reasons we cannot accept change-of-mind returns of pierced earrings, underwear, swimwear without its hygiene liner, or beauty items once their seal is broken. Faulty items are always covered.

## Faulty, damaged or wrong items

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, goods must be as described, fit for purpose and of satisfactory quality. If something is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will give you a full refund, including delivery, or a replacement if you prefer, and we cover the cost of returning the item. After 30 days you are still entitled to a repair or replacement, or a refund if that is not possible.

## Exchanges

To exchange an item for a different size or colour, return it for a refund and place a new order.
