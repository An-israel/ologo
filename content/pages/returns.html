If you change your mind, you can return unworn items within 14 days of receiving them. This is in addition to your legal rights.

How to start a return

  1. Contact us with your order number and the items you want to return.
  2. We reply with return instructions and the address to send your parcel to.
  3. Pack the items unworn, unwashed and with their labels attached, and keep your proof of postage.

Refunds

We refund you to your original payment method within 14 days of receiving your return. If you return a whole order within 14 days, we also refund the standard delivery charge you paid.

Items we cannot take back

For hygiene reasons we cannot accept returns of pierced earrings, underwear or opened beauty items, unless they are faulty.

Faulty or wrong items

If something arrives faulty, damaged or not as described, contact us within 30 days with your order number and a photo. We will offer a refund or a replacement and cover the cost of sending it back. Your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 are not affected.

Exchanges

The quickest way to get a different size or colour is to return the original item for a refund and place a new order.
