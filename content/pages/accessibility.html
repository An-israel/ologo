We want everyone to be able to shop at Ornis, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, voice control or screen magnification.

The standard we aim for

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We test our shop with automated tools, by keyboard only and with VoiceOver on iPhone.

What you can do on this site

  • use every page with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline
  • zoom up to 200% without losing content
  • read text with good colour contrast
  • turn off animation by setting "reduce motion" on your device

Known limitations

Checkout and payment pages are provided by Shopify, and some features such as the reviews widget and Klarna messages come from third-party services that we do not fully control.

Tell us about a problem

If you find something hard to use, please contact us. Tell us the page and what went wrong, and we will reply and help you complete your order another way if needed.
