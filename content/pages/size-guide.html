Find your size in the charts below. Measurements are of the body, in centimetres with inches in brackets. Each product page also has its own size chart and notes on how that item fits.

How to measure

  • Chest or bust: measure around the fullest part, keeping the tape level under your arms.
  • Waist: measure around your natural waistline, the narrowest part of your body.
  • Hips: stand with your feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips.
  • Length: measured on the garment from the top of the shoulder to the hem.

If you are between sizes, choose the larger size for a relaxed fit or the smaller size for a closer fit.
