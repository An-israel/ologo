Ornis is a small independent online shop selling affordable fashion for women, men and children, plus beauty accessories and gifts. Most of what we sell costs between £10 and £35.

How we choose products

We look for well-made, good-value pieces and check each one before it goes on sale. Every product must:

  • come from a supplier with a strong track record and real photos from buyers
  • have a proper size chart, so you can choose the right fit
  • reach UK customers within a reasonable time
  • be legal to sell in the UK. We do not list cosmetics, supplements or electricals unless they meet UK safety rules

We write our own descriptions and only list fabric and care details that the supplier has confirmed.

Where your order comes from

Ornis works directly with a small number of suppliers. Most items are sent straight from their warehouses, some of which are outside the UK, so items in one order can arrive in separate parcels. Every product page shows an estimated delivery date before you buy.

Honest prices

We only show a "was" price when an item really sold at that price first. You will not see countdown timers or made-up stock warnings here, and every review comes from a verified buyer.
