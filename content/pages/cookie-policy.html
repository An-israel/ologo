This page explains the cookies we use on ornis.shop and how you can control them.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files saved on your device when you visit a website. Some are needed for the site to work. Others help us understand how the site is used or show you relevant adverts.

Cookies we use

  • Essential: set by Shopify to keep your bag, run checkout and keep the site secure. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics: Shopify Analytics and Google Analytics help us see which pages are useful. Only set if you accept.
  • Marketing: Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok measure whether our adverts work. Only set if you accept.

Your choice

When you first visit, our cookie banner lets you accept, reject or manage cookies. Analytics and marketing cookies are only used after you accept. You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of every page, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

For more on how we use personal data, read our privacy policy.
