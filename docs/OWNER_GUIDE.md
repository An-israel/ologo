# Owner guide

You run the money and the customers. Isaiah, the operator, runs the products and the website. You never need to edit code or listings.

## Your daily routine (about 15 minutes)

1. **Check new orders.** Open the Shopify app, then Orders. Each new order is placed with the supplier automatically. If an order shows a red "High risk" label, do not fulfil it. Message Isaiah.
2. **Check payouts.** Open Finances > Payouts. Money from Shopify Payments arrives in your business bank account every few working days.
3. **Answer customer messages.** Open the Inbox app (website chat) and your support email. Try to reply within one working day.

That is it. Isaiah gets an automatic alert if an order has not shipped within 3 working days.

## Common questions from customers

| The customer says | What you do |
|---|---|
| "Where is my parcel?" | Open the order and copy the tracking link from Fulfilment. Send it to the customer. If the latest delivery date has passed, message Isaiah to chase the supplier. |
| "I only got part of my order" | Items can come from different warehouses and arrive separately. Check the order: each fulfilment has its own tracking link. |
| "I want to return something" | Check it is within 14 days of delivery and the item is unworn. Reply with the return instructions from the Returns page. Refund once it arrives back (see below). |
| "It arrived faulty or wrong" | Ask for a photo and the order number. Offer a refund or replacement. The customer does not pay to send it back. |
| "Can I change my order?" | If it is not yet fulfilled, Isaiah can change it. Otherwise treat it as a return. |

## How to issue a refund

1. Orders > open the order > **Refund**.
2. Tick the items, choose whether to refund delivery (always refund it if the whole order is returned within 14 days), and add a short reason.
3. Press **Refund**. The money goes back to the customer's original payment method, and Shopify emails them.

## When to call Isaiah

- the website looks broken or a page shows strange text
- a product is wrong, sold out at the supplier, or priced wrongly
- a chargeback (payment dispute) arrives. Do not reply to it alone
- anything you are not sure about

Isaiah aims to reply within 4 hours during the day for urgent problems. For less urgent issues, he replies within one working day.

## Things only you can do

- approve the launch (Isaiah will send you a preview link to check on your phone)
- confirm business facts: legal name, address, VAT decision, delivery times
- billing: your Shopify plan and app charges come out of your account
